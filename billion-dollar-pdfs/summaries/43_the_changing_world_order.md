## Summary

Paul Graham's essays (paulgraham.com) form the intellectual core of Y Combinator. Key essays: 'Do Things That Don't Scale,' 'How to Get Startup Ideas,' 'Startup = Growth,' 'Black Swan Farming,' 'The Equity Equation,' 'Founder Vesting,' 'Ramen Profitable.' Philosophy: startups are search for repeatable scalable business model; growth is the only metric; founders should live in the future.

## Key Takeaways
- Startup = search for repeatable scalable model
- Growth is the only metric that matters
- Do things that don't scale (manual → automated)
- Organic ideas from own problems > synthetic brainstorming
- Equity: standard vesting, avoid premature optimization
- Ramen profitability = survival = optionality

## Capital Impact
YC produced 3000+ companies ($600B+ valuation): Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, DoorDash, Coinbase, Instacart, Reddit, Cruise. Graham's essays are the most-read startup literature globally.

## Related Mental Models
Growth as metric, organic ideas, manual→automated, ramen profitability, black swan farming
