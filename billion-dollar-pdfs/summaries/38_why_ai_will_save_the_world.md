## Summary

Marc Andreessen (2023): AI augments human intelligence and improves nearly everything; doom fears are misplaced. Historical pattern: new tech (printing press, electricity, internet) feared → saves lives, creates wealth. AI = universal problem solver (healthcare, education, science, governance). Regulation should accelerate deployment, not restrict. 'AI doomerism' is a luxury belief of comfortable elites.

## Key Takeaways
- AI = intelligence amplifier; intelligence solves problems
- Every prior tech panic was wrong (Luddites, Y2K, etc.)
- AI doctor/tutor/scientist for every person
- Regulation should be 'permissionless innovation'
- Doom scenarios assume static defense; miss adaptive offense

## Capital Impact
a16z's public stance on AI regulation. Countered Pause AI letter. Influenced policymakers (Schumer AI forums). Framed AI as abundance engine, not existential risk.

## Related Mental Models
