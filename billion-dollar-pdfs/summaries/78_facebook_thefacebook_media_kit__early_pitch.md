## Summary

Sahil Lavingia (Gumroad, 2011-present): Raised VC, failed, bought back investors, bootstrapped to $10M+ ARR. 'No meetings, no deadlines, no managers.' Async everything; write things down. Creators sell directly to fans. 'The creator economy is the economy.' Profit-sharing with team. 'We don't optimize for growth; we optimize for survival and sanity.'

## Key Takeaways
- No meetings, no deadlines, no managers
- Async writing culture; decisions in docs
- Creator economy: direct fan monetization
- Profit-sharing aligns team
- Survival + sanity > growth at all costs
- Bought back VC; bootstrapped to profitability

## Capital Impact
Gumroad $10M+ ARR, profitable, team of 20+. Lavingia's 'no meetings' model adopted by Linear, Levels, others. Creator economy thesis ($100B+ market) validated. Gumroad creator payouts $500M+.

## Related Mental Models
No meetings, async writing, creator economy, profit-sharing, survival over growth
