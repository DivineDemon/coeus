## Summary

Kevin Kelly (2008, updated 2017): Creators need only 1,000 true fans paying ~$100/year = $100K living. True fan = buys everything, drives 200 miles, alerts on. Direct relationship (no intermediaries) keeps full $100. Web enables direct connection + long tail (niche finds fans). Crowdfunding (Kickstarter, Patreon) operationalizes this. True fans also market to lesser fans. Alternative to stardom: sustainable, saner, more likely.

## Key Takeaways
- 1,000 true fans × $100 = $100K/year living
- Direct relationship = keep 100% vs 10-15% via intermediaries
- Long tail: 1-in-a-million appeal = 7,000 people globally
- Crowdfunding = pre-financing from true fans
- True fans amplify to lesser fans (concentric circles)
- Saner destiny than lottery of stardom

## Capital Impact
Enabled creator economy ($100B+). Patreon ($4B+ valuation), Kickstarter ($2.5B funded), Substack, OnlyFans, Gumroad. Shifted media from ad-supported to fan-supported. Empowered niche creators globally.

## Related Mental Models
Creator economy, direct-to-consumer, long tail, crowdfunding, micro-patronage
