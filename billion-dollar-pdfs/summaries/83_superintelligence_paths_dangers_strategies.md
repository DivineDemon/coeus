## Summary

Sam Altman (OpenAI, YC 2014-2019): Compound self-improvement. Work on important problems. Take risks early. Focus: do few things exceptionally well. Build network: help people. Think long-term (10+ years). 'Compound interest applies to careers.' Sales skills > technical skills for founders. 'Hard work compounds.'

## Key Takeaways

- Important problems + risk early + focus
- Network: help people without expectation
- Think 10+ years
- Sales > technical for founders
- Hard work compounds
