## Summary

Michael Mauboussin (Credit Suisse, Morgan Stanley, 2000s-present): Stock price = market's expectations of future cash flows. Investing = finding gaps between price and expectations. 'Expectations Investing' (2001, updated 2021): reverse DCF to find implied expectations; assess if realistic. Base rate thinking: use reference class forecasting. Skill vs luck: outcomes = skill + luck; process > outcome. 'The Success Equation' quantifies luck's role.

## Key Takeaways
- Price = expectations; value = reality; edge = gap
- Reverse DCF: what expectations are priced in?
- Base rates: use reference class, not single case
- Process > outcome (luck contaminates outcomes)
- Mauboussin's 'checklist' for investment decisions

## Capital Impact
Institutional investing curriculum. Mauboussin's reports trained generations of analysts. 'Expectations Investing' is required reading at L/S funds. Counterpoint to 'value investing' — focuses on expectations revision.
