## Summary

Clayton Christensen (1997): Great firms fail because they listen to customers and invest in sustaining innovations. Disruptive innovations: worse on mainstream metrics, better on new ones (cheaper, simpler, accessible). Incumbents rationally ignore (low margin, small market). Entrants move upmarket; incumbents flee upmarket. Eventually disruptor meets mainstream needs. 'Jobs to be Done': customers 'hire' products for jobs.

## Key Takeaways
- Sustaining: better products for existing customers
- Disruptive: worse on old metrics, better on new (cheaper/simpler)
- Incumbents rationally ignore; resource allocation processes filter out disruption
- Entrants move upmarket; incumbents retreat
- Jobs to be Done: understand the 'job' not the product

## Capital Impact
Defined 'disruption' vocabulary. Influenced Intel (Celeron), Apple (iPhone disrupted iPod), Netflix (disrupted Blockbuster), Tesla (disrupting auto). Christensen's framework used by every corporate strategy team and VC.

## Related Mental Models
Sustaining vs disruptive, rational ignorance, upmarket migration, jobs to be done, resource allocation process
