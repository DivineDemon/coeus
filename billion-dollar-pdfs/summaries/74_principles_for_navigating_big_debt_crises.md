# 74. Principles for Navigating Big Debt Crises

- **Thesis**: Debt crises follow a repeating, mechanical template of leveraging and deleveraging that can be navigated.

## Summary

Brent Beshore (Permanent Equity, 2010s): Buy wonderful businesses, hold forever. No fund lifecycle (no 10-year forced sales). Focus: boring, essential, cash-generating (lawn care, HVAC, niche manufacturing). Owner earnings > EBITDA. Culture preservation. 'We don't flip; we compound.' Aligned incentives: GP co-invests heavily, no management fees on committed capital.

## Key Takeaways
- Permanent capital: no fund lifecycle, no forced sales
- Boring essential businesses: pricing power, recurring revenue
- Owner earnings > EBITDA (capex matters)
- Culture preservation = value preservation
- GP alignment: co-invest, no fees on committed
- Compound forever; don't flip

## Capital Impact
Permanent Equity model copied by HoldCo movement (Berkshire-style for SMBs). Tiny Capital, Xenon, others. Shifted private equity from '3-5 year flip' to 'hold forever' for quality SMBs.

## Related Mental Models
Permanent capital, hold forever, boring businesses, owner earnings, GP alignment, compounding
