## Summary

Ben Horowitz (a16z, 2014): There's no formula for hard decisions. Wartime CEO vs Peacetime CEO: wartime = survival focus, direct, decisive; peacetime = growth focus, consensus, strategic. 'The Struggle' = when you don't know what to do. Manage psychology (yours and team's). Fire fast; hire for strength not lack of weakness. 'Take care of the people, the products, and the profits — in that order.'

## Key Takeaways
- No formula for hard things; judgment in the moment
- Wartime CEO: survival, direct, decisive; Peacetime: growth, consensus
- The Struggle is where greatness is forged
- Hire for strength; fire for fit
- Culture = what you tolerate
- Take care of people → products → profits

## Capital Impact
a16z co-founder; book is CEO bible. Opsware sold to HP for $1.6B. a16z portfolio: Facebook, Airbnb, Coinbase, Slack, GitHub. Horowitz's 'wartime CEO' framing used by every founder in crisis.
