# 23. All Markets Are Not Created Equal / Note to Self on TAM

- **Thesis**: Ten factors determine whether a digital marketplace can become large and durable; not all TAMs are equal.

## Summary

Bill Gurley (Benchmark, 2012): 10 factors for evaluating digital marketplaces. 1) Experience delta vs status quo 2) Economic advantage both sides 3) Tech adds value 4) High fragmentation 5) Low supplier signup friction 6) TAM + penetration likelihood 7) Market expansion potential 8) High frequency 9) Payment flow control 10) Network effects. Score 7-8/10 = high success probability; 3-4 = low. Execution matters more than analysis.

## Key Takeaways
- Marketplace success = pull on BOTH consumer AND supplier sides
- Experience delta creates 'wow' → viral growth
- Fragmented suppliers = easier aggregation; concentrated = fight
- TAM analysis must include penetration probability
- Network effects are 'magic' — get stronger with scale
- Being in payment flow = economics extraction + supplier alignment

## Capital Impact
Benchmark's marketplace thesis (eBay, OpenTable, Yelp, Zillow, GrubHub, Uber, 1stdibs, UShip). Gurley's framework used by every marketplace investor. $100B+ in marketplace value creation.

## Related Mental Models
Marketplace dynamics, supply/demand aggregation, network effects, TAM penetration, payment flow
