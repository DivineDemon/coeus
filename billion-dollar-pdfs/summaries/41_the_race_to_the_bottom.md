# 41. The Race to the Bottom

- **Thesis**: Investors competing to accept ever-worse terms and lower returns are sowing the seeds of the next crisis.

## Summary

Bill Gurley (Benchmark) codified marketplace evaluation in 'All Markets Are Not Created Equal' and subsequent posts. 10 factors: experience delta, economic advantage both sides, tech adds value, high fragmentation, low supplier friction, TAM+penetration, expansion potential, high frequency, payment flow control, network effects. Scorecard approach: 7-8/10 = likely winner. Execution > analysis. 'Being in the payment flow is everything.'

## Key Takeaways
- 10-factor scorecard for marketplace TAM assessment
- Experience delta drives viral consumer pull
- Payment flow = economic extraction + supplier alignment
- Network effects are the 'magic' compounder
- High frequency + fragmentation = easier aggregation

## Capital Impact
Benchmark's marketplace portfolio: eBay, OpenTable, Yelp, Zillow, GrubHub, Uber, 1stdibs, UShip. Gurley's framework is the industry standard for marketplace investing.

## Related Mental Models
Marketplace scorecard, payment flow economics, network effects magic, frequency x fragmentation
