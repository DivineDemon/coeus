## Summary

Howard Marks' quarterly memos (1990-present) on market cycles, risk, second-level thinking. Key memos: 'The Most Important Thing' (2003), 'Sea Change' (2013), 'Race to the Bottom' (2019), 'Something of Value' (2020). Themes: cycles are inevitable; risk = permanent loss of capital (not volatility); second-level thinking beats consensus; invest when others are fearful; 'this time is different' is the most dangerous phrase.

## Key Takeaways
- Market cycles: excess → correction → excess
- Risk = permanent capital loss, not volatility
- Second-level thinking: what does market miss?
- Invest at maximum pessimism (counter-cyclical)
- 'This time is different' = danger signal
- Patient opportunism > constant activity

## Capital Impact
Oaktree $150B+ AUM, top distressed debt returns. Marks' memos are required reading at every major institution. 'The Most Important Thing' book is value investing canon.
