## Summary

Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO, 2024): Powerful AI could compress century of scientific progress into a decade. Biology/medicine: cure cancer, Alzheimer's, extend healthspan. Neuroscience: understand brain, treat mental illness. Economic development: bring global south to developed standards. Peace: AI-mediated governance, reduce conflict. Requires: alignment, security, equitable access. Not utopia — but best shot at solving civilization's hardest problems.

## Key Takeaways
- AI compresses 100 years of science → 10 years
- Biology: cure major diseases, extend healthy lifespan
- Neuroscience: treat depression, anxiety, PTSD at root
- Economic: leapfrog development for global south
- Guardrails: alignment, security, access, governance

## Capital Impact
Anthropic's mission statement ($18B valuation). Influenced AI safety funding (NTIA, UK AISI). Framed alignment as enabling abundance, not just preventing harm. Guides Anthropic's R&D priorities.

## Related Mental Models
AI for science, compressed progress, alignment as enabler, equitable AI, bio/medical AI
