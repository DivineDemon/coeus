## Summary

Jensen Huang (NVIDIA, 1993-present): GPU computing = accelerated computing = new industrial revolution. CUDA (2006) made GPUs programmable; AlexNet (2012) proved deep learning on GPU. 'The more you buy, the more you save' (per-unit compute cost drops). Blackwell (2024): 208B transistors, 4x training, 30x inference. AI factories = data centers generating intelligence. 'We are at the iPhone moment of AI.'

## Key Takeaways
- Accelerated computing: GPU + CUDA + libraries = new compute paradigm
- More buy = more save (economics of scale in AI)
- AI factories: data centers produce intelligence tokens
- Full stack: chips → systems → software → models
- Huang's Law: GPU compute doubling < 2 years (vs Moore's 2 years)
- 'iPhone moment of AI' = mass adoption inflection

## Capital Impact
NVIDIA $3T+ market cap (from $10B in 2015). Created $100B+ AI chip market. Huang's keynotes move markets; Blackwell pre-orders sold out. NVIDIA is the 'arms dealer' of AI gold rush.
