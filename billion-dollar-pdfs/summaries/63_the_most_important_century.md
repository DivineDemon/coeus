## Summary

Mark Zuckerberg (Meta, 2022-2023): 'Year of Efficiency' after 2022 crash (-70% stock). Flatten org: remove middle managers, increase IC ratio. Cut 'low priority' projects (Portal, NFTs, Live Shopping). Invest in AI infrastructure (GPU clusters). Threads launch (100M users in 5 days). Open source Llama (strategic: commoditize model layer, own distribution). 'We're in the business of building the best products, not the most products.'

## Key Takeaways
- Year of Efficiency: flatten, cut, focus
- IC ratio up: managers → ICs or leave
- Cut non-core; double down on AI + core apps
- Threads: speed of execution (5 days to 100M)
- Open source Llama: commoditize competitors' moat
- 'Best products, not most products'

## Capital Impact
Meta $300B → $1.3T+ recovery. AI infra spend $35B+/yr. Llama 3 = open source SOTA. Threads = Twitter/X competitor. Zuckerberg's crisis management is case study in founder-led turnaround.
