## Summary

Marc Andreessen (2011 WSJ): Every company becomes a software company. Software firms disrupt incumbents across all industries. Drivers: 2B broadband users, smartphones, cloud (100x cost drop), open source, SaaS. Examples: books (Amazon), video (Netflix), music (Spotify), recruiting (LinkedIn), telecom (Skype), cars (Tesla), retail (Walmart logistics), finance (Square), healthcare, education, defense. US structural advantages: universities, risk culture, equity capital, rule of law.

## Key Takeaways
- Software delivers 100x cost reduction
- Every industry becomes software-mediated
- Winner-take-all favors SV-style startups
- US advantages: research, risk culture, capital, law
- Challenges: headwinds, talent, stranded workers

## Capital Impact
Defined 2010s venture thesis. Directed $500B+ VC to software disruption. a16z portfolio: Facebook, Airbnb, Coinbase, Slack, GitHub, Figma, Roblox, OpenAI.

## Related Mental Models
Software-mediated disruption, zero marginal cost, winner-take-all, US structural advantage
