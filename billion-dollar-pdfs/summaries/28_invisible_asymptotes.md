## Summary

Eugene Wei (2018): Every growth curve has an invisible ceiling. Great companies identify and route around theirs before hitting it. Twitter's asymptote: 140-char limit constrained power users; solved by threads, images, video. Facebook's: real-name policy limited pseudonymity; solved by Groups, Pages. Amazon's: shipping cost/speed; solved by Prime, logistics. Identify your asymptote early; build the bridge before you hit the wall.

## Key Takeaways
- Every product has a growth ceiling (asymptote)
- Asymptotes are often non-obvious (culture, policy, physics)
- Best companies see them early and build bridges
- Twitter: 140 chars → threads/media; Facebook: real names → Groups
- Amazon: shipping → Prime/logistics; Netflix: DVD → streaming

## Capital Impact
Product strategy framework for Facebook, Airbnb, Stripe, Notion. Wei's Amazon/Airbnb experience informed this. Used to diagnose growth stalls and prioritize roadmap.
