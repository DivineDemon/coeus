## Summary

Daniel Vassallo (ex-AWS, 2020s): Don't bet everything on one startup. Make many small bets (products, content, investments). 'Small Bets' philosophy: low downside, uncapped upside. Build audience; launch micro-products; learn fast. 'The portfolio approach to entrepreneurship.' No single point of failure. Financial independence via diversified income streams.

## Key Takeaways
- Portfolio of small bets > all-in on one startup
- Low downside, uncapped upside per bet
- Build audience first; launch micro-products
- Diversified income = financial independence
- No single point of failure
- Learn fast; iterate; kill losers quickly

## Capital Impact
Vassallo's 'Small Bets' course/community (1000+ students). Influenced indie hackers to diversify. 'Portfolio entrepreneurship' is alternative to VC-backed swing-for-fences. Financial independence via multiple small cash flows.

## Related Mental Models
Portfolio entrepreneurship, small bets, audience-first, diversified income, financial independence
