## Summary

Lyn Alden (2020s): Fiscal dominance = fiscal policy drives monetary policy. High debt/GDP forces central banks to cap yields (financial repression). Result: structural inflation, negative real rates, currency debasement. Hard assets (energy, commodities, bitcoin) outperform. 'The 1940s playbook' — US 1940s: war debt → yield curve control → inflation → financial repression. Current trajectory rhymes.

## Key Takeaways
- Fiscal dominance: fiscal > monetary policy
- Debt/GDP > 120% → yield curve control → inflation
- 1940s playbook: financial repression, negative real rates
- Hard assets outperform financial assets
- Bitcoin as digital hard asset / monetary hedge
- Currency debasement = wealth transfer from savers to debtors

## Capital Impact
Alden's framework guided retail/institutional allocation to energy/commodities/bitcoin 2020-2024. 'Fiscal dominance' entered mainstream macro discourse. Lyn Alden Investment Strategy newsletter widely followed.
