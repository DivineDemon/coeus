## Summary

Reed Hastings (Netflix, 2009 culture deck, 2020 book): Freedom & responsibility > process. 'Context not control': set strategy, let team decide tactics. Keeper test: would you fight to keep this person? If no, fire generously. Top of market pay (no bonuses). No vacation/expense policies. Candor: feedback anytime, anywhere. High talent density enables low process. 'Adequate performance gets generous severance.'

## Key Takeaways
- Freedom & responsibility: high talent density = low process
- Context not control: align on strategy, decentralize tactics
- Keeper test: fight to keep? If no → generous exit
- Top of market pay; no bonuses (salary = market)
- Radical candor: feedback as gift
- Adequate performance = not good enough

## Capital Impact
Netflix $250B+ peak. Culture deck downloaded 20M+ times. Influenced Spotify, HubSpot, Shopify, Stripe. 'Keeper test' and 'context not control' are standard management vocabulary.
