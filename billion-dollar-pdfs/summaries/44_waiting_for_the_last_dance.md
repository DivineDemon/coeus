## Summary

Sequoia's classic founder guidance: clarity of thought, market size, differentiation, team, business model. 'DNA of an Entrepreneur' (Don Valentine): clarity, leadership, market instinct, persistence. 'Writing a Business Plan': problem, solution, market, competition, model, team, financials, risks. Sequoia's filter: large market, unique insight, exceptional team, defensible moat.

## Key Takeaways
- Clarity of thought > complex financial models
- Large market + unique insight + great team = Sequoia filter
- Don Valentine: 'We don't fund ideas; we fund people'
- Business plan = thinking tool, not prediction document
- Persistence through near-death experiences = founder DNA

## Capital Impact
Sequoia portfolio: Apple, Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Airbnb, Stripe, NVIDIA, Zoom, ServiceNow. $10T+ in portfolio value. Valentine/Moritz/Roelof lineage defined modern venture.

## Related Mental Models
Founder DNA, clarity of thought, large market thesis, persistence, business plan as thinking tool
