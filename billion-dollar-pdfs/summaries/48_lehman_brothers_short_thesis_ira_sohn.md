## Summary

Ray Dalio (Bridgewater, 2021): Empires rise and fall in 250-year cycles driven by debt, currency, internal conflict, external war. 6 stages: new order → resource allocation → peace/prosperity → excess/debt → conflict → revolution/war → new order. US in late stage (high debt, polarization, rising China). Diversify across asset classes, countries, currencies. 'Holy Grail' portfolio: 15+ uncorrelated returns.

## Key Takeaways
- Big cycle: 250 years; debt cycle: 50-75 years
- 6 stages from new order to revolution/war
- US: high debt, polarization, China rising
- Diversify: 15+ uncorrelated return streams
- Gold/bitcoin as currency hedges
- Study history: patterns repeat with variations

## Capital Impact
Bridgewater $150B+ AUM, largest hedge fund. Dalio's framework guides sovereign wealth funds, central banks. 'Principles' and 'Changing World Order' are macro investing bibles.
