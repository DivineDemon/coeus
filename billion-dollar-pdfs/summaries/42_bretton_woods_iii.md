## Summary

Blake Masters' notes from Thiel's Stanford CS183 (2012) became 'Zero to One.' Covers: monopoly vs competition, secrets, power law, distribution, definite optimism, last mover advantage, foundations. 'Every moment in business happens only once.' The next Bill Gates won't build an OS; next Larry Page won't build a search engine. Copying = 1 to n; creating = 0 to 1.

## Key Takeaways
- Monopoly = value capture; competition = value destruction
- Secrets exist; find them in nature or people
- Power law: one investment returns the fund
- Distribution is not a detail; it's the business
- Definite optimism: future is knowable and shapeable
- Foundations: co-founder alignment, culture, board

## Capital Impact
Founders Fund thesis; Thiel's track record (Facebook, Palantir, SpaceX, Airbnb, Stripe). Book sold 2M+ copies. Required reading in Silicon Valley.
