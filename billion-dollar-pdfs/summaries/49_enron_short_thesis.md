## Summary

Stan Druckenmiller (Duquesne, Soros protégé): Markets are reflexive — participant beliefs affect fundamentals which affect beliefs. No equilibrium; boom/bust driven by feedback loops. Trade the narrative shift, not the static value. 'It's not whether you're right or wrong; it's how much you make when right and lose when wrong.' Concentrated bets (1-3 positions = 80% of portfolio). Cut losses instantly; let winners run.

## Key Takeaways
- Reflexivity: beliefs ↔ fundamentals feedback loop
- No equilibrium; markets trend to extremes
- Trade the narrative inflection point
- Concentrated conviction bets (1-3 positions)
- Risk management: cut losses, ride winners
- 'How much you make when right' > win rate
