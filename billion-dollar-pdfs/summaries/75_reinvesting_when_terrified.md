## Summary

Codie Sanchez (Contrarian Thinking, 2020s): Buy boring cash-flowing businesses (laundromats, car washes, vending, HVAC, plumbing). Seller financing + SBA loans = low equity down. 'Main Street' wealth creation ignored by Wall Street. Demographics: boomer retirement = $10T+ business succession wave. Systems/operators > owner-operators. 'Cash flow is king; appreciation is queen.'

## Key Takeaways
- Boring businesses: essential, recurring, fragmented
- Seller financing + SBA = low equity, high leverage
- Boomer succession: $10T+ wave (silver tsunami)
- Systems + operators > owner-operators (scale)
- Cash flow first; appreciation second
- Contrarian: Wall Street ignores Main Street

## Capital Impact
Contrarian Thinking newsletter 500K+ subscribers. Sparked 'boring business' acquisition trend. SBA 7(a) loan volumes surged. Created new asset class: 'Main Street private equity' for individuals.

## Related Mental Models
Boring businesses, seller financing, SBA loans, silver tsunami, systems over owner-operators
