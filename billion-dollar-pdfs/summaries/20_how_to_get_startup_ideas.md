## Summary

Paul Graham (2013): Best startup ideas are things founders themselves want that sound like bad ideas but are good. Live in the future and build what's missing. Don't 'try to think of startup ideas' — notice problems in your own life. Organic ideas (from own experience) beat synthetic (brainstormed). Look for 'schlep blindness' (hard problems people avoid) and 'unsexy' markets. The best ideas are often initially dismissed.

## Key Takeaways
- Organic > synthetic ideas; solve your own problems
- Live in the future; build what's missing
- Bad-sounding ideas = less competition
- Schlep blindness: hard/unsexy problems are opportunities
- Notice what's broken in your daily workflow

## Capital Impact
YC curriculum; generated companies like Airbnb (founders needed rent money), Dropbox (forgot USB drive), Stripe (painful payments). Framework used by thousands of founders.
