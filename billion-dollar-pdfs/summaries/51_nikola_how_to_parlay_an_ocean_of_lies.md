## Summary

Hindenburg Research's September 2020 report exposed Nikola Corporation as an "intricate fraud." The electric truck startup, valued at $30B+ at peak (exceeding Ford), had no working prototype. Key deceptions: the "Nikola One" semi truck video showed it rolling downhill (not under its own power); the "Badger" pickup was a Ford F-150 with Nikola badges; hydrogen station claims were false; Bosch partnership overstated. Founder Trevor Milton resigned; SEC/DOJ investigations followed; Milton convicted of fraud (2022). The report demonstrated forensic short-selling's power to expose fraud in SPAC/hype markets.

## Key Takeaways
- Nikola One video: truck rolling downhill, not self-propelled
- No working hydrogen fuel cell truck existed
- Badger pickup = rebadged Ford F-150
- SPAC merger (VectoIQ) bypassed IPO scrutiny
- Trevor Milton convicted of securities/wire fraud (2022)

## Capital Impact
Nikola peak market cap: $30B+ (June 2020). Hindenburg report triggered 60%+ collapse. Short sellers profited ~$1B+. Case study in SPAC due diligence failure; led to stricter SEC SPAC rules. Demonstrated forensic research value.

## Related Mental Models
Forensic short selling, SPAC risks, hype vs reality, due diligence, fraud detection
