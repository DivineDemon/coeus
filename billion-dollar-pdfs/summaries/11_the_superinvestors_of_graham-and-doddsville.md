## Summary

Warren Buffett (1984) essay refuting Efficient Market Hypothesis. Nine investors from Graham-Newman 'village' (Buffett, Schloss, Tweedy Browne, Ruane Cunniff, etc.) achieved 20-30% annual returns over decades with concentrated value portfolios. Shared intellectual framework: buy $1 for 40c, margin of safety, circle of competence. Statistical impossibility under EMH proves markets aren't efficient.

## Key Takeaways
- Concentrated value investing by disciples of Graham beats market consistently
- Margin of safety (price << intrinsic value) is the core principle
- Circle of competence: only invest where you have edge
- Temperament > intellect for investment success
- EMH fails because humans are emotional, not rational

## Capital Impact
Intellectual foundation for $10T+ value investing industry (Berkshire ~$1T, plus disciples' firms). Trained generations of allocators; Buffett's letters are required reading at every major fund.
