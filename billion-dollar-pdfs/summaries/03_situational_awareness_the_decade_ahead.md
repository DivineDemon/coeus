## Summary

Leopold Aschenbrenner (2024) argues AGI by ~2027 and superintelligence shortly after, based on trendlines in compute (0.5 OOMs/year), algorithmic efficiency (0.5 OOMs/year), and 'unhobbling' gains (chatbot → agent). Projects a trillion-dollar compute buildout, US-China race, and inevitable government AGI project ('The Project') by 2027-28. Covers security risks (labs leaking secrets to CCP), superalignment challenge, and the Free World's survival stakes.

## Key Takeaways
- GPT-2→GPT-4 = preschooler→smart high schooler in 4 years; expect similar jump to AGI by 2027
- Intelligence explosion: millions of AGIs automating AI research → decade of progress in ≤1 year
- Trillions in GPU/datacenter/power investment this decade; US electricity +10s of percent
- Current lab security inadequate for state-actor threats
- Superalignment (controlling >>human AI) is unsolved but solvable; failure = catastrophic

## Capital Impact
Reframed AI investment thesis from 'hype cycle' to 'industrial mobilization.' Influenced trillion-dollar capex plans at hyperscalers, sovereign wealth fund allocations, and government policy (CHIPS Act, AI executive orders).

## Related Mental Models
Scaling laws, intelligence explosion, compute governance, AI safety, great power competition
