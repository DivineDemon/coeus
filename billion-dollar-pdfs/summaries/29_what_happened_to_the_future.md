## Summary

Founders Fund / Bruce Gibney (2011): Venture abandoned hard technology for trivial consumer apps. 'We wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters.' Critique of VC focus on social/mobile/adtech vs energy, biotech, space, AI. Hard tech has longer horizons but civilization-level impact. Founders Fund created to fund 'hard startups' (SpaceX, Palantir, DeepMind, etc.).

## Key Takeaways
- VC shifted from atoms to bits (easier, faster returns)
- Hard tech: longer cycles, higher capital, but existential impact
- 'Flying cars vs 140 characters' = civilization stagnation
- Founders Fund thesis: back hard tech with long time horizons
- Government withdrew from R&D; private capital must fill gap

## Capital Impact
Defined Founders Fund strategy ($10B+ AUM). Investments: SpaceX, Palantir, Anduril, DeepMind, Airbnb, Stripe. Inspired 'American Dynamism' thesis (a16z) and hard tech renaissance.

## Related Mental Models
Hard tech vs consumer tech, venture time horizons, civilization stagnation, government R&D withdrawal
