# 67. We Need a New Science of Progress

- **Thesis**: We should study the causes of scientific and economic progress as a rigorous discipline to accelerate it.

## Summary

Nassim Taleb (2007-2018): Antifragile > robust > fragile. Fragile breaks under stress; robust resists; antifragile gains from disorder. Black Swans: rare, extreme impact, retrospective predictability. Skin in the game: decision-makers must bear downside. Via negativa: remove harm (subtract) > add good. Barbell strategy: 90% safe + 10% speculative (no middle). Lindy effect: future life expectancy = current age.

## Key Takeaways
- Antifragile: gains from volatility/stress/disorder
- Black Swans: unpredictable, massive impact, explainable ex-post
- Skin in the game: no downside transfer
- Via negativa: subtract harm (simpler than adding)
- Barbell: extreme safety + extreme risk, no medium
- Lindy: non-perishable things age in reverse

## Capital Impact
Taleb's frameworks adopted by risk managers, central banks, tail hedging funds. Universa Investments (Taleb's fund) returns 100x+ in crashes. 'Antifragile' and 'Skin in the Game' are modern risk philosophy canon.

## Related Mental Models
Antifragility, black swans, skin in the game, via negativa, barbell strategy, Lindy effect
