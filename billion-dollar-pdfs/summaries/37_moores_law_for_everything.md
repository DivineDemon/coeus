## Summary

Sam Altman (2021): AI will drive cost of goods and labor toward zero, requiring new social contract. Compute cost drops exponentially; AI automates cognitive labor; robotics automates physical labor. Proposes: tax capital (not labor), distribute via UBI/universal basic services. 'The American Equity Fund' — tax companies 2.5% in equity, land 2.5% in value, distribute to citizens. Transition from labor economy to capital economy.

## Key Takeaways
- AI + robotics → marginal cost of intelligence & labor → 0
- Labor share of GDP collapses; capital share → 100%
- Tax capital/land, not labor; distribute as dividend
- 'American Equity Fund': 2.5% equity + 2.5% land tax → UBI
- Social contract must evolve: from 'work for income' to 'own for income'

## Capital Impact
Altman's (OpenAI CEO) policy framework. Influenced UBI pilots, AI taxation debates, sovereign wealth fund discussions. Framed AI economics as political economy question, not just tech.

## Related Mental Models
AI economics, UBI, capital vs labor, automation displacement, social contract
