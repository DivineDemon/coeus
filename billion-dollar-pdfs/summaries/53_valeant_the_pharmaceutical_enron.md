## Summary

Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern, 1980s-present): 'Valuation is not modeling; it's disciplined thinking.' Free lectures on YouTube (millions of views). DCF, relative valuation, option pricing, narrative-to-numbers. 'Numbers without narrative are meaningless; narrative without numbers is fantasy.' Country risk, corporate governance, ESG integration. The 'Dean of Valuation' teaches that every number must have a story.

## Key Takeaways
- Valuation = narrative + numbers
- DCF: cash flows, growth, risk → intrinsic value
- Relative valuation: multiples reflect market mood
- Options: real options in growth/shutdown/expansion
- Story must be plausible, consistent, and testable
- Bias: we overvalue what we understand/own
