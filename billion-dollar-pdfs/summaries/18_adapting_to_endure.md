## Summary

Sequoia Capital (2022) presentation: era of free capital over; founders must pivot to profitability as rates rise and multiples compress. Public market SaaS multiples dropped 60%+; private markets lag. Advice: extend runway to 36 months, cut burn, prioritize core product, raise if possible but don't count on it. 'Default dead' companies must become 'default alive.' Focus on unit economics, not growth at all costs.

## Key Takeaways
- Rate regime change: ZIRP → 5%+ Fed funds; valuation gravity returns
- Runway > growth; profitability = optionality
- Private markets reprice with 6-12 month lag to public
- Best founders act early; delayed action = death spiral
- Crisis = opportunity: talent available, competitors distracted, entry prices lower

## Capital Impact
Guided Sequoia portfolio through 2022-2023 reset; template for all VCs. Companies that adapted (e.g., Ramp, Notion) thrived; those that didn't shut down or down-rounded.

## Related Mental Models
Runway management, unit economics, default alive/dead, counter-cyclical hiring, valuation gravity
