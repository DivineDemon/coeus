## Summary

Reid Hoffman (2016 HBR): Prioritize speed over efficiency in winner-take-all markets. Accept chaos, fires, technical debt to achieve massive scale before competitors. Stages: Family → Tribe → Village → City → Nation. Key: raise more than needed, hire aggressively, launch imperfect products. Counterintuitive: inefficiency is the strategy. Only works in markets with strong network effects and winner-take-all dynamics.

## Key Takeaways
- Speed > efficiency when first-mover advantage is decisive
- 5 stages of scaling: Family(1-9) → Tribe(10s) → Village(100s) → City(1000s) → Nation(10000s)
- Raise capital aggressively; cash = speed
- Hire for trajectory, not current role
- Only for winner-take-all markets with network effects

## Capital Impact
Defined hypergrowth playbook for Uber, Airbnb, Slack, Stripe, Coinbase. Justified massive burn rates ($1B+ rounds). Counterpoint: 'Adapting to Endure' (2022) reversed this for rate-hike era.

## Related Mental Models
Winner-take-all, network effects, hypergrowth, capital as weapon, growth vs efficiency
