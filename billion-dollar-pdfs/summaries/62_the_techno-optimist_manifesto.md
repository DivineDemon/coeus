## Summary

Page & Brin (2004-2015): 'Don't be evil.' 10x thinking: aim for 10x improvement, not 10%. 'Healthy disregard for the impossible.' Focus on user, all else follows. Organize world's information. Moonshots: X (Waymo, Loon, Wing, Verily). Alphabet structure: bets outside core. 'We want to build technology that makes a difference in the world.'

## Key Takeaways
- 10x thinking: 10% is incremental; 10x forces first principles
- Healthy disregard for the impossible
- User focus = long-term value
- Moonshots: portfolio of 10x bets (X)
- Alphabet: separate core from bets
- Don't be evil (early north star)

## Capital Impact
Google/Alphabet $2T+ market cap. Created search advertising ($200B+/yr). Moonshots: Waymo ($30B+), Verily, DeepMind. 10x thinking is Silicon Valley gospel.

## Related Mental Models
