## Summary

Brian Chesky (Airbnb, 2024): 'Founder mode' vs 'manager mode.' Conventional wisdom: hire executives, delegate. Reality: founders must stay in details (product, design, recruiting, crisis). Chesky returned to reviewing every pixel, interviewing ICs, writing specs. 'The details are the product.' Manager mode = abstraction layers = loss of context = mediocrity. Founder mode = intensity, context, taste, speed.

## Key Takeaways
- Founder mode: stay in details (product, design, people)
- Manager mode: delegate via hierarchy = context loss
- Details = product; abstraction = mediocrity
- Founder's taste/speed/intensity = competitive advantage
- Crisis reveals: Chesky led COVID pivot personally
- Board/investors often push manager mode (wrong)

## Capital Impact
Airbnb $100B+ market cap. Chesky's 2024 essay sparked 'founder mode' debate. Validated by Jobs (Apple), Musk (Tesla/SpaceX), Zuckerberg (Meta), Collison (Stripe). Counter-narrative to professional CEO doctrine.
