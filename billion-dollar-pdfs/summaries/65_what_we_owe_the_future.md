## Summary

Warren Buffett (1977-present): 50+ years of annual letters. Circle of competence. Margin of safety. Intrinsic value > market price. Quality businesses at fair prices > fair businesses at wonderful prices. Moats: pricing power, switching costs, network effects. Long-term holding (forever preferred). 'Be fearful when others are greedy; greedy when others are fearful.' Buybacks when below intrinsic value.

## Key Takeaways
- Circle of competence: only invest what you understand
- Margin of safety: price << intrinsic value
- Quality > cheap (see's Candies lesson)
- Moats: pricing power, switching costs, brands
- Forever holding period; compounding works magic
- Fear/greed contrarian timing
- Buybacks = tax-efficient shareholder return

## Capital Impact
Berkshire $1T+ market cap. 20%+ CAGR for 50+ years. Letters are value investing bible. Buffett's framework trained every major allocator. 'Quality at fair price' shifted value investing from cigar-butts to compounders.

## Related Mental Models
Circle of competence, margin of safety, quality > cheap, moats, forever holding, contrarian timing
