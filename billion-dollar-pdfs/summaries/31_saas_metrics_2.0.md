## Summary

David Skok (forEntrepreneurs, 2010s): Definitive dashboard of SaaS unit economics. Key metrics: CAC (customer acquisition cost), LTV (lifetime value), LTV:CAC ratio (>3x), payback period (<12 months), churn (logo & revenue), net revenue retention (>100%), magic number (sales efficiency), ARR/MRR growth. Rules of thumb for each stage. The 'SaaS metrics bible' for founders and investors.

## Key Takeaways
- LTV:CAC > 3x; payback < 12 months = healthy
- Net revenue retention > 100% = expansion > churn
- Magic number = (Q revenue growth × 4) / prior Q sales spend
- Churn is the silent killer; negative churn = best
- Unit economics must work before scaling spend

## Capital Impact
Standardized SaaS investing; used by every VC and public market analyst. Enabled $500B+ SaaS market cap. Skok's blog is required reading for SaaS founders.
