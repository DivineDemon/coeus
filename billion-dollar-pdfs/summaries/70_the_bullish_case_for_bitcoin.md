## Summary

Meb Faber (Cambria, 2010s): Global asset allocation = diversify across assets, geographies, strategies. Ivy Portfolio: endowment model (stocks, bonds, real assets, alternatives) for individuals. Trend following: 10-month MA as risk management. 'Buy cheap, sell expensive' via valuation (CAPE). Shareholder yield (dividends + buybacks) > dividend yield. 'The best portfolio is the one you can stick with.'

## Key Takeaways
- Global diversification: assets + geographies + strategies
- Ivy Portfolio: endowment model for individuals
- Trend following: 10-month MA = risk off
- Valuation matters: CAPE predicts 10-year returns
- Shareholder yield = dividends + buybacks + debt paydown
- Stick-with-able > theoretically optimal

## Capital Impact
