# 56. Mobile Is Eating the World

## Summary

Jeff Bezos (1997-2021): 24 annual letters distill Amazon's mental models. Day 1 philosophy: always act like a startup. Two-way doors (reversible) vs one-way doors (irreversible). High-velocity decisions. Customer obsession > competitor focus. 'Your margin is my opportunity.' Flywheel: lower prices → more customers → more sellers → better selection → lower prices. Long-term thinking (7-year horizons). Disagree and commit.

## Key Takeaways
- Day 1: maintain startup urgency at scale
- Two-way doors: decide fast, reverse if wrong
- Customer obsession: start from customer, work backward
- Flywheel: self-reinforcing loop of selection/price/traffic
- Long-term: 7-year horizons; willing to be misunderstood
- Disagree and commit: debate hard, execute united

## Capital Impact
Amazon $1.5T+ market cap. Letters are most-studied corporate documents. Flywheel model copied by every platform. 'Two-way doors' = decision framework for Google, Meta, startups. Bezos' Blue Origin applies same models to space.
