## Summary

Sequoia Capital (2008) presentation to portfolio companies: the 2008 crash is structural, not cyclical. 'Cut burn, extend runway, get to cash-flow positive.' Advised: reduce headcount, cut marketing, renegotiate leases, raise capital NOW at any terms, focus on core product. 'Cash is king.' The good times of easy capital are dead; survival requires brutal prioritization.

## Key Takeaways
- Structural reset: valuations, fundraising, exit markets all broken
- Extend runway to 24+ months; profitability > growth
- Raise when you can, not when you need to
- Focus on must-have products; cut nice-to-haves
- Downturns create best entry prices for investors (Sequoia deployed heavily 2008-2010)

## Capital Impact
Saved countless portfolio companies; Sequoia's 2008-2010 vintage became one of best in history (WhatsApp, Instagram, Airbnb, Stripe). Template for every subsequent downturn (COVID 2020, 2022 tech crash).

## Related Mental Models
Runway management, counter-cyclical investing, burn rate discipline, structural vs cyclical
