# 66. AGI Ruin: A List of Lethalities

## Summary

Charlie Munger (1995 Harvard talk): 25 cognitive biases that distort judgment. 1) Reward/punishment superresponse 2) Liking/loving 3) Disliking/hating 4) Doubt avoidance 5) Inconsistency avoidance 6) Curiosity 7) Kantian fairness 8) Envy/jealousy 9) Reciprocation 10) Influence from mere association 11) Simple pain-avoiding denial 12) Excessive self-regard 13) Over-optimism 14) Deprival super-reaction 15) Social proof 16) Contrast misreaction 17) Stress influence 18) Availability misweighing 19) Use-it-or-lose-it 20) Drug-misinfluence 21) Senescence-misinfluence 22) Authority-misinfluence 23) Twaddle tendency 24) Reason-respecting 25) Lollapalooza (multiple biases combining).

## Key Takeaways
- 25 biases systematically distort decisions
- Lollapalooza: multiple biases combine for extreme outcomes
- Incentives (bias 1) are the most powerful
- Social proof (15) drives bubbles and crashes
- Inversion: avoid stupidity by knowing biases
- Checklists prevent bias-driven errors

## Capital Impact
Berkshire's discipline via bias awareness. Munger's talk is the most-cited behavioral finance lecture. Influenced Kahneman (Nobel), Thaler (Nobel), every decision-making framework. Checklists now standard in investing, medicine, aviation.

## Related Mental Models
25 cognitive biases, lollapalooza effect, inversion, incentive superresponse, social proof, checklists
