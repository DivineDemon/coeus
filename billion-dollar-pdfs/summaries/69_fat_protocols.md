## Summary

Joel Greenblatt (Gotham Capital, 2005): Magic Formula = rank stocks by (1) earnings yield (EBIT/EV) and (2) return on capital (EBIT/(net fixed assets + working capital)). Buy top 20-30; hold 1 year; rebalance. Simple, mechanical, beats 95% of pros. 'Good companies at bargain prices.' Works because it's systematic and unemotional. Gotham returned 40%+ annual for 20 years.

## Key Takeaways
- Magic Formula: Earnings Yield + Return on Capital
- Mechanical: rank, buy top, hold 1 year, repeat
- Beats 95% of professionals
- Systematic > discretionary (removes emotion)
- Good business (high ROC) + cheap (high yield)
- 20-30 stocks for diversification

## Capital Impact
Gotham Capital 40%+ CAGR 20 years. Formula widely tested; persists due to behavioral resistance (boring, requires patience). Greenblatt's Columbia class spawned value investing generation. 'Little Book' sold 1M+ copies.

## Related Mental Models
Magic formula, earnings yield, return on capital, mechanical investing, behavioral resistance
