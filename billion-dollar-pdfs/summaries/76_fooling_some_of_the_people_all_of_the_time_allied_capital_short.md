## Summary

Nathan Barry (ConvertKit, 2013-present): Bootstrapped to $30M+ ARR. 'Teach everything you know' → audience → product. Public build: share revenue, metrics, failures. Creator economy focus (email for creators). No VC; profitable from year 1. 'Charge more'; value-based pricing. Hire slow; document everything. 'The audience is the moat.'

## Key Takeaways
- Teach → audience → product (not product → audience)
- Public building: transparency builds trust
- Creator niche: email marketing for creators
- Bootstrapped: profitable from day 1, no dilution
- Charge more; value-based pricing
- Audience = moat; document everything

## Capital Impact
ConvertKit $30M+ ARR, profitable, no VC. Barry's playbook inspired 1000s of bootstrapped founders. 'Audience-first' is now standard SaaS strategy. ConvertKit valued $100M+ on secondary.

## Related Mental Models
Audience-first, public building, bootstrapped SaaS, creator economy, value-based pricing
