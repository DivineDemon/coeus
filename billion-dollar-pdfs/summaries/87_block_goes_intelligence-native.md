## Summary

Morgan Housel (2020 book, Collaborative Fund): Wealth is what you don't see. Saving rate > investment returns. Compounding requires time, not brilliance. 'Tails drive everything' (few events = most outcomes). Room for error = survival. 'Reasonable > rational.' History is study of change, not guide to future. 'The highest form of wealth is waking up and saying: I can do whatever I want today.'

## Key Takeaways
- Wealth = what you don't see (savings, not spending)
- Saving rate > returns (control what you control)
- Compounding = time × consistency
- Tails drive outcomes (few events = most impact)
- Room for error = survival
- Reasonable > rational (sustainable behavior)
- Freedom = highest wealth
