## Summary

Elon Musk (2006) outlined Tesla's top-down strategy: 1) Build expensive low-volume sports car (Roadster) 2) Use proceeds to fund mid-volume sedan (Model S) 3) Use proceeds to fund affordable high-volume car (Model 3) 4) Provide solar power (SolarCity). The master plan reframed EVs from 'compromise' to 'aspirational,' proving demand at each tier before scaling down.

## Key Takeaways
- Start at high end where early adopters pay premium for performance
- Each generation funds the next; no external capital dependency at scale
- Vertical integration (batteries, software, charging, manufacturing) creates moats
- Mission-driven narrative attracts talent, capital, and customer loyalty
- Solar + storage + transport = complete sustainable energy ecosystem

## Capital Impact
Tesla became world's most valuable automaker (~$1T peak), catalyzed global EV transition ($1T+ industry), forced legacy auto into $500B+ EV capex, created battery supply chain, made Musk world's richest person.

## Related Mental Models
