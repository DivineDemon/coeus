## Summary

Patrick Collison (Stripe, 2010s-present): 'Fast' organization model. Write things down; async by default. Small teams (2-pizza). High writing culture (memos > meetings). 'Fast' = low latency decisions. 'Slow' = committees, approvals, meetings. Stripe's operating principles: users first, move with urgency, think rigorously, trust and amplify. 'The most important decisions are reversible.'

## Key Takeaways
- Async writing > sync meetings for decisions
- Small autonomous teams (2-pizza)
- Writing culture: memos force clarity
- Low latency decisions = competitive advantage
- Reversible decisions: decide fast, fix if wrong
- Trust and amplify: hire great people, get out of way

## Capital Impact
Stripe $50B+ valuation. Collison's writing on organizational velocity widely cited. Stripe's operating principles adopted by Linear, Notion, Vercel. 'Fast' model is the modern alternative to BigCo bureaucracy.

## Related Mental Models
Async first, writing culture, small teams, decision latency, reversible decisions, trust and amplify
