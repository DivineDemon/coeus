## Thesis
Simulation fidelity is improving fast. Trust is not.
World models and robotics simulators are shipping faster than anyone can independently audit them. Haga is the evaluation layer that exposes physics failures before deployment.

## What we are
Haga is the verification layer for physical AI.

We evaluate two failure points with one adversarial method:
- Robot policy: does it survive physical stress?
- Generated world: is the physics consistent?

Output: reproducible numeric reports with defined thresholds and documented failure cases.
