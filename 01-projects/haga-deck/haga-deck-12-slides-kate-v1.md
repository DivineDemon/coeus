## Slide 2 — Problem

**Headline:** Simulated success does not survive the real world.

- 89.4% sim success vs 12% real household-task success.
- Labs grade themselves; generated worlds ship without an independent physics check.
- Small teams lack a fast, private pre-check before expensive hardware evals.

**Consequence:** Physics-trust gap compounds as sims scale.

---

## Slide 3 — Why now

**Headline:** Physical-AI capital is flooding; independent verification is still missing.

- Capital: $27.6B physical AI / robotics across 1,009 deals in 2025.
- World models becoming training fuel; physics mistakes compound downstream.
- Standards pressure: collaborative robot applications need documented validation.

**Consequence:** Verification has a buyer, a timeline, and a budget line — none of which existed 18 months ago.

---

## Slide 4 — Solution

**Headline:** You send a policy or generated world. We break it in simulation.

- Robot policy → stress-test → curven/weak results.
- Generated world → physics-consistency checker → violation report.
- Output: numeric score, failure cases, clear thresholds.

**Consequence:** A private pre-check that runs inside existing sim/release workflows.

Mockup concept:
- Left: policy simulator running Lift/Stack/PickPlaceCan under easy → hard conditions.
- Right: generated-world checker with teleportation/anti-gravity/interpenetration flags.
