## Outreach Strategy by Tier

### Tier 1 VCs (Lux, Playground, Eclipse, Refactor, DCVC)
- **Goal**: Secure seed round ($500k-$2M SAFE) or strategic investment
- **Approach**: Lead with traction, team, market size, and defensive moat
- **Materials**: Pitch deck, demo video, term sheet (SAFE), cap table
- **Ask**: Intro to partners, request meeting to discuss investment

### Tier 2 Angels & Syndicates
- **Goal**: Fill out round with strategic angels who can add value
- **Approach**: Personal connection, emphasize upside and EIS/SEIS tax benefits (if UK-based eventually)
- **Materials**: One-pager, demo video, simple terms
- **Ask**: "Can I send you a quick overview?" then follow up with details

### Tier 3 Corporate Venture Arms
- **Goal**: Secure pilot, partnership, or strategic investment
- **Approach**: Lead with specific use case relevant to their portfolio/industry
- **Materials**: Use case-specific one-pager, pilot proposal
- **Ask**: "Can we explore a 3-month paid pilot to verify [specific use case]?"

### Tier 4 Simulation/Engineering Companies
- **Goal**: Secure licensing deal, OEM partnership, or integration project
- **Approach**: Lead with technical integration benefits and market access
- **Materials**: Technical API docs, integration guide, pilot proposal
- **Ask**: "Can we discuss embedding Haga's verification as a plugin/service in your platform?"

### Tier 5 Video Generation/WORLD MODEL Companies
- **Goal**: Secure verification contract or partnership
- **Approach**: Lead with risk reduction and compliance benefits
- **Materials**: Compliance framework one-pager, sample verification report
- **Ask**: "Can we run a free verification on your latest model to show value?"

### Tier 6 AV/Robotics Companies
- **Goal**: Secure safety verification contract or partnership
- **Approach**: Lead with safety case, regulatory prep, recall prevention
- **Materials**: Safety case framework, ISO 21448/SOTIF relevance
- **Ask**: "Can we help you build a safety case for your next-gen system?"
