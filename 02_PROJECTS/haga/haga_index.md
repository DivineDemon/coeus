# Haga Enterprise Hub

> **The Bet**: Build the independent trust and verification layer that robotics and physical-AI companies urgently need for policy stress-testing and physics-consistency checks.

---

## Architecture & Codebases

1. **[haga-core](file:///Users/mushood/Documents/code/haga/haga-core)**
   - **Pillar 1 (Policy Stress)**: Mass & friction degradation curves on MuJoCo / Robosuite.
   - **Pillar 2 (World-Model Checker)**: Physics violation detection (teleportation, hovering, impulse, interpenetration) across video & simulation data (CoTracker, CogVideoX).
   - **Lab Sync**: Publishes metrics JSON to `artifacts/public/` for site/data room.

2. **[haga-web](file:///Users/mushood/Documents/code/haga/haga-web)**
   - Public Lab ([/lab](https://haga.mushoodhanif.com/lab)) & Methodology.
   - Private Data Room for investor diligence.
